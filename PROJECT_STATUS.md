## 2. Part I — The Model (this is close to submission-ready)

**Theoretical backbone:** OERA's generic 5-stage model — Direction → Enable → Discover → Act → Learn & Scale — each instantiated by one evidence-grounded ITC capability:

| Stage | Applied capability | Questions | What it probes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direction | Anticipatory Innovation | 3 | Proactive vs. reactive planning for future demand |
| Enable | Risk Tolerance & Psychological Safety | 3 | Can ITC safely pilot and fail small? |
| Discover | Ecosystem Stability | 3 | Do partnerships hold under funding/talent competition? |
| Act | Scalability ("Accordion") | 3 | Can IT/procedures expand and contract under crisis load? |
| Learn & Scale | Knowledge Management | 3 | Is specialized trauma expertise captured, not lost? |

**Scoring method:** every question rated **Current** (1–5) and **Target** (1–5). Priority = Target × (5 − Current). Innovation-barrier flag: Current avg < 3.0 **or** gap ≥ 1.5. Stakeholder-divergence flag: ≥1.5-point spread between HQ Leadership / Regional Field Coordinators / Member NGO Leaders on the same capability.

**Justification lineage to cite:** draws on INSA (Knowledge Management is literally one of its 15 sub-categories; Ecosystem Stability maps to its Innovation Ecosystem category) and the Innovation Map (Anticipatory Innovation maps to the internal/external, sustaining/breakthrough axis) — adapted into your own instrument per the assignment's requirement, not adopted wholesale.
