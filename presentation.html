What's working

The good

🟢 Ecosystem Stability is a real strength

  • Highest-rated capability, closest to its own target — the smallest current→target gap of all 5
  • Consistent with real evidence: ITC coordinates 58 member organizations and has run a formal government partnership since 2005
  • This is a foundation to build on, not a risk to manage

🟢 Knowledge intent exists, even if execution lags

  • Second-highest current score (3.96) — staff and leadership both recognize the need to capture trauma expertise
  • The gap here is about systematizing an instinct that already exists, not building one from nothing
What's at risk

The bad

🔴 Scalability is the #1 priority gap

  • Lowest current score (3.04) and the widest gap to target (1.89 pts) — the single highest-priority finding in the whole model
  • Reads as: crisis surges are handled by improvisation, not documented procedure — a real risk when institutional memory walks out the door

🟠 A leadership-vs-field disconnect, hypothesized

  • Simulated Executive respondents consistently rate Risk Tolerance and Scalability higher than Field/Resilience-Center respondents
  • Unconfirmed — this is exactly what Wednesday's interview needs to test with a real HQ voice and a real field voice
One-year outlook

Three priority moves

1

Document the surge playbook

Turn tribal-knowledge onboarding/IT procedures into a written, rehearsed surge protocol — piloted in one region first.

90-day MVC · Owner: Ops lead
2

Protect planning time from crisis mode

A standing, protected working session for post-ceasefire and future-demand scenarios — not squeezed in around emergencies.

Quarterly cadence · Owner: Leadership
3

Validate the HQ–field gap, then close it

Confirm the divergence with real interviews first; if real, open a direct two-way channel between HQ and Resilience Center directors.

First 90 days · Owner: All layers
Where this leaves us

This is a rehearsal, not a diagnosis

Every number on the last four slides came from 12 invented personas. The real work starts Wednesday, testing exactly these three things with real ITC voices.
