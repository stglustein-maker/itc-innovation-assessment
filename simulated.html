ORGANIZATIONAL ENTREPRENEURIAL READINESS ASSESSMENT SIMULATED RESPONSES

ITC multi-level readiness profile

This standalone site demonstrates how the Live Assessment's results could look after a real multi-level organizational survey. It uses the same 5 capabilities and 15 questions as the Live Assessment, filled in with 12 synthetic respondent personas across executive, program, field and operations layers.

IMPORTANT

The respondent ratings are desk-research hypotheses for presentation purposes. They are not actual answers provided by ITC staff.

01 · OVERVIEW

Simulated organizational profile

The output emphasizes the pattern across roles rather than only one overall score.

Current state by capability

Strongest organizational signal

Ecosystem Stability. The simulated respondents consistently rate ITC's ability to maintain partnerships and mobilize its member-NGO network as a major strength.

Primary bottleneck

Scalability ("Accordion"). The pattern suggests ITC may be better at sensing and mobilizing partners than at rapidly deploying the IT infrastructure and procedures a crisis surge actually requires.
